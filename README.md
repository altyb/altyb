# Altayeb

**Full-stack developer.** I take a product from idea to live — design, database, backend, front end,
deployment, and the maintenance after launch. One person, no hand-offs.

Self-taught. Everything below was learned by shipping it.

## What I build

- **Commerce** — storefronts, checkout, admin panels, and the money flows behind them. Card rails,
  or on-chain USDT where card processors don't reach.
- **Web apps** — dashboards, booking, inventory, internal tools, with roles and permissions that
  hold as a team grows.
- **Mobile** — Flutter for Android and iOS: camera, on-device computer vision with OpenCV in Dart,
  NFC, offline-first storage, signed store releases.
- **The parts nobody sees** — multi-tenant architecture, idempotent payments, background jobs and
  retries, schema migrations, deploys that roll back.

## Selected work

**[FilmBox](https://github.com/altyb/FilmBox)** — film and series discovery on the TMDB API.
Every filter, sort and page lives in the URL, so a filtered view is a link you can send someone.
One request per title page instead of eight. Bilingual English and Arabic, switching language
refetches in place rather than reloading. React · TypeScript · Vite · TanStack Query.

Most of my work is client software under agreement — written up at
**[altayeb.dev](https://altayeb.dev)**, at the level of what it guarantees rather than how it is
wired.
